About Me

I am a PhD Scholar at the University of Science and Technology (UST) / Korea Institute of Ocean Science and Technology (KIOST), Busan, South Korea. My research interests span Artificial Intelligence, Coastal Engineering , Anomaly Detection , and Ocean Data Analysis. I focus on edge AI, Marine Wave Anomaly Detection, Fog Monitoring and Physics-Guided Multi-Task Learning.
